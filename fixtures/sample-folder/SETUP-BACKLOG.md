# SETUP BACKLOG

Everything the secretary should be able to do and can't yet. Added the moment it's mentioned —
the whole point is that it gets captured before it's forgotten, not that it gets built today.

**DONE never gets pruned.** It's the record of what this thing has actually become.

---

## NOW — build one of these next

- **Nothing rolls over on Monday.** Friday's unfinished items just vanish from the board instead
  of being carried, closed or explicitly dropped. Mentioned 2026-03-02.
- **The newsletter has no lane of its own in the brief.** It's lane 3 in MASTER-PLAN but never
  appears on the board, which is why missing 5 March went unnoticed for four days.
- **No way to add a reminder without opening the JSON.** Sam has done it by hand four times and
  broke the file once. Mentioned 2026-02-25.

## NEXT

- **Invoice status should come from somewhere real.** `invoices_outstanding` is typed in by hand
  and has been wrong twice.
- **A weekly review that writes itself.** The Friday 17:00 reminder fires and Sam looks at the
  log — the system could draft the answer to "what moved, what rotted" from the log itself.
- **Application tracking.** Applications are counted but not tracked — no record of which company,
  which date, what came back. The board holds it manually and it leaks.
- **Search across the folder.** Finding "what did I say to Dana about the portfolio" means
  grepping by hand.

## LATER

- **A people lane.** Everything is a task lane. Nothing holds *people* — who is owed a
  conversation, when they last spoke, what about. Marcus Ellery sat unanswered for a week
  because he was a person, not a task, and nothing owned him.
- **Phone capture.** Screenshots, links and voice notes on the phone have no route into the folder.
- **A monthly retro.** Which lanes actually got time versus which lanes were claimed as priorities.
  Uncomfortable in a useful way.
- **Read-only share link.** One URL showing a partner or a coach only what Sam chooses.
