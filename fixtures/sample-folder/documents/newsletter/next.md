## Working title

The paragraph that should have been a table

## The argument

Most documentation that is hard to read is not badly written. It is prose doing a table's job.
The test is whether a reader ever needs to hold two sentences in their head at once to compare
two things. If they do, it was a table.

## What goes in it

- The three-column test, and where it breaks down
- Why "it reads better as prose" is usually an author-side preference, not a reader-side one
- One before-and-after, from public documentation, not from client work
